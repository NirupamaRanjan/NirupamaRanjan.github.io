About

After a few years of working in data analytics at ICICI Bank, I decided to switch gears and dive deeper into technology. I’m now pursuing an M.Tech in Computer Science at IIIT Hyderabad. Basically, I traded spreadsheets for code (and somehow ended up with even more debugging). Outside of tech, I’m also into painting and dancing, so I like to think I have at least a few creative outlets when my code refuses to cooperate.

Currently, I’m exploring systems programming, databases, and machine learning, and I’m especially excited about building things from scratch, whether it’s a shell, a smart text-matching system, or just another project that gives me a reason to ask, “Why is this segfaulting?”

Work Experience

Data Scientist

June 2022 – August 2025

ICICI Bank Private Limited, Hyderabad

  • During my role at ICICI Bank, I worked extensively with large-scale financial transaction data to solve business problems related to salary identification, transaction categorization, and customer analysis. I designed SQL-based algorithms to identify salary transactions from millions of banking records, achieving around 95% accuracy across 10M+ records. I also worked on categorizing 500K+ daily transactions based on their nature and business context. The analysis helped the business identify relevant customer segments and use transaction-level information for generating reports and extending financial facilities. I also automated several recurring data-processing and reporting workflows, significantly reducing the amount of manual effort required from the team.
  • I also worked on improving the identification and matching of company and organization names in incoming banking data. I developed a Python-based NLP text-matching solution to match noisy or differently formatted company names against existing records. The solution processed 100K+ incoming records and improved the matching coverage from approximately 30% to 50%, making it possible to identify a significantly larger number of relevant companies automatically. In addition, I automated reporting workflows using SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI, reducing monthly report-generation time from around 48 hours to less than 1 hour and reducing certain manual tasks from approximately 2 hours to 2 minutes.
